Good company.
Simple controls.

  1. Look around. Move your cursor inside the blue stage.
  2. Say hello. Tap Milo’s face, or choose Wave.
  3. Make a little moment. Try Dance, Sleep/Wake or Reset.

On your phone, use the buttons beneath Milo. You can use Tab and Enter to reach the controls, too.
